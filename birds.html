Bird care

A bright, social life for your bird

Companion birds are intelligent and highly social. Thoughtful cage setup, a varied diet, and daily interaction keep them healthy and happy.

A safe, roomy cage

Space to move and climb is vital to a bird's wellbeing.

  • Choose the largest cage you can, with bar spacing suited to your species.
  • Offer varied perch sizes and textures.
  • Place the cage in a social but calm area, away from the kitchen.
  • Provide daily out-of-cage time in a bird-safe room.

A varied, balanced diet

Seeds alone are not enough for most companion birds.

  • Base the diet on quality pellets suited to your species.
  • Add fresh vegetables and small amounts of fruit.
  • Avoid avocado, chocolate, and caffeine — all toxic to birds.
  • Refresh food and water daily.

Daily mental enrichment

Bored birds can develop stress behaviors — keep minds busy.

  • Rotate foraging toys and puzzles regularly.
  • Spend time talking and interacting each day.
  • Offer safe items to chew and shred.
  • Maintain a consistent light and sleep schedule.
