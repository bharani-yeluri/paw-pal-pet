Cat care

A calm, enriched life for your cat

Cats thrive on routine, clean spaces, and gentle enrichment. Here's how to keep yours healthy, playful, and content indoors.

Care by life stage

Choose your cat's stage

Select a stage for focused, age-appropriate guidance.

Kitten (0–12 months)

Curiosity and rapid growth call for safety and gentle handling.

  • Feed kitten-formula food in frequent small meals.
  • Introduce the litter box early and keep it spotless.
  • Offer safe toys for pouncing and chasing.
  • Handle gently and often to build trust.

Adult (1–10 years)

Enrichment and routine keep an indoor cat balanced.

  • Measured meals; monitor weight to avoid obesity.
  • Daily interactive play to satisfy hunting instincts.
  • Provide vertical space and scratching posts.
  • Annual vet visits and dental care.

Senior (10+ years)

Comfort, accessibility, and monitoring matter most.

  • Senior diet with easy access to food and water.
  • Low-sided litter boxes for easier entry.
  • Warm, quiet resting spots.
  • Twice-yearly checkups for kidney and thyroid health.
