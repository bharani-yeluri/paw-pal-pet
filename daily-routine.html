Daily routine

A routine your pet can actually live with.

You do not need a military schedule. You need repeatable anchors for food, movement, enrichment, care, and rest.

Build a pet plan
Example day

Use this as a framework, not a cage.

Morning

Food + toilet + movement

Start with the basics, then give your pet a chance to move and explore before a longer rest period.

Midday

Enrichment + check-in

Use a puzzle, sniffing activity, forage setup, short training session, or calm interaction.

Evening

Exercise + connection

Choose species-appropriate activity, followed by dinner and a predictable wind-down period.

Night

Quiet + sleep

Keep the final hour boring. A consistent sleep environment helps many pets settle faster.
