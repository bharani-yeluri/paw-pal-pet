Grooming & coat care

Calm, confident grooming

Regular grooming keeps skin and coat healthy, reduces shedding, and gives you a chance to spot problems early — all while strengthening your bond.

Get a grooming planBuild trust first

Brushing: the foundation

Brushing removes loose fur, prevents mats, and distributes natural oils for a healthy shine. Frequency depends on coat type — long or double coats may need daily attention, while short coats do well with a weekly session.

Bathing without the battle

Most pets need bathing only occasionally, using a species-appropriate shampoo. Use lukewarm water, keep it calm and brief, and reward generously. Over-bathing can strip natural oils and dry out skin.

Go slow: Introduce grooming tools gradually and pair each new step with treats and praise so your pet learns the routine is safe and rewarding.

Nails, ears, and teeth

  • Trim nails before they touch the ground or click loudly — avoid the sensitive quick
  • Check ears for redness, odor, or discharge and clean only as advised
  • Introduce tooth brushing early with pet-safe toothpaste
  • Watch for lumps, flaky skin, or parasites while you groom
