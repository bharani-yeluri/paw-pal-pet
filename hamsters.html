Small pet care

Small pets, big personalities

Hamsters and other small pets need the right habitat, a proper diet, and gentle handling. Here's how to keep your little companion thriving.

Lifespan 2–3 yrsMostly nocturnalNeeds a big habitatSolitary (Syrian)

Choose a care area

Small pets have specific essentials. Explore each one.

A spacious, deep habitat

More floor space and deep bedding for burrowing is key.

  • Provide the largest enclosure you can with deep, safe bedding.
  • Add a solid-surface wheel large enough to prevent a curved back.
  • Include hideouts, tunnels, and chew items.
  • Keep the habitat away from direct sun and drafts.

A balanced small-pet diet

A quality mix plus fresh extras keeps them healthy.

  • Offer a species-appropriate commercial mix or pellets.
  • Add small amounts of fresh vegetables.
  • Provide safe items to gnaw for dental health.
  • Always supply fresh water via bottle or bowl.

Gentle, patient handling

Let trust build slowly, especially with nocturnal pets.

  • Handle during waking hours, never startle a sleeping pet.
  • Scoop with two cupped hands close to a surface.
  • Keep early sessions short and calm.
  • Use treats to build positive associations.

Good to know

Common small pet questions

Syrian hamsters are solitary and must live alone to avoid serious fighting. Some dwarf species can live in pairs under specific conditions, but always research your species first.
Choose a solid-surface wheel large enough that your pet can run without arching its back. A too-small wheel can cause spinal strain over time.
Most small pets like hamsters are naturally nocturnal or crepuscular. Interact during their waking hours in the evening and avoid waking them during the day.
